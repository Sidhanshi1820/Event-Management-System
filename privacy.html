Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information We Collect

When you create an EventNov@ account we collect your name, email address, company name and phone number. When you request a proposal we collect booking details such as event type, event date and expected guest count. We also keep a login history (IP address, user agent and timestamps) to protect your account.

How We Use It

We use your data to run the service, respond to your proposal requests and manage your bookings. We also use it to secure your account and send service communications such as booking confirmations and event updates.

Cookies & Local Storage

We store session and authentication tokens in your browser's local storage so you stay logged in between visits. These tokens are used only for login persistence and are cleared when you sign out. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

Data Sharing

Your personal data is never sold. We share it only with the event vendors (venues, caterers, AV teams and similar partners) as needed to deliver the event you booked, and only the details required for that purpose.

Data Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. All traffic between your browser and our servers is encrypted in transit, and authentication tokens are revoked when you log out.

Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account data only for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account via the dashboard, your account data is removed from our systems, except where a longer retention period is required by law.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you at any time. Email info@eventova.com and we will respond promptly.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach our privacy team at info@eventova.com and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
