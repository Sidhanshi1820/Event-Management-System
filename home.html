Frequently Asked Questions

Everything corporate event organizers usually ask us.

How much does a corporate event cost?

Seminars, workshops and webinars typically start from ₹35,000–₹75,000. Conferences, product launches and galas usually range from ₹1,50,000 to ₹10,00,000+ depending on scale, venue and production. Every quote is itemized — no hidden charges.

Do you organize only corporate events?

Yes — exclusively. We handle all types of corporate events (conferences, seminars, product launches, trade shows, galas, retreats, team building and more) and deliberately don't take on weddings or private parties, so our entire team and vendor network is corporate-focused.

How far in advance should we book?

For workshops, webinars and team events, 3–4 weeks is usually enough. For large conferences, trade shows and product launches we recommend 3+ months so you get the best venues and production slots.

Do you handle virtual and hybrid formats?

Yes. We produce webinars, fully virtual conferences and hybrid events with live streaming, virtual attendee engagement tools and integrated AV for in-room and online audiences.

Which locations do you serve?

We organize corporate events pan-India across 25+ cities — including Delhi NCR (Noida, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Delhi), Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Chennai — plus fully virtual events anywhere.

How do I get a quote?

Click Get Started, create your free account and submit the booking form with your requirements. Our team responds within 24 hours with a detailed proposal and pricing.
